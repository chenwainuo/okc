## Design (what wins)
Swap-AB: each CTA owns 128 W rows × all m tokens; two math warpgroups (64 rows each) run register-A wgmma (m64 n=NT k16) with A
decoded from the 12-bit pack in the math warps (dec1 formulation, ~0.9 ALU ops/weight); x is the smem B operand via 2D TMA
(box NT tokens × 64 k, 128B swizzle); K split over a one-wave grid (pooler 32 row-tiles × KS4 = 128 CTAs; qkv 18 × 7 = 126;
unpooler 64 × 2; o 64 × 2 = 128 with only 8 k64 rounds each — why o loses); producer warpgroup with separate W and x lanes;
one merged full barrier per slot; k64 rounds; ring depth 5 (ST5); L2 hints evict-first W / evict-last x (box-only gain,
neutral on H200); in-kernel zeroing of the output (no memset node); escape lists (weights outside the 12-bit window, ~1e-4)
loaded before the W flood and applied as exact fp32 fixups; epilogue = cp.reduce.async.bulk.tensor bf16 add of each CTA's
partial (one TMA reduce-add per CTA, 128×NT bf16 slab). 168 registers (launch-bounds cap), 0 spills at NT=128 and 256.

## m=256: closed for this design (19:00 UTC)
Resident rate at NT=256 is 1027–1033 clk per k64 round vs the 1024 floor (100% of tensor peak) for both n256 and the two-pass
n128×2 variant, but full kernels are 25.6 kernel-µs with x from HBM and no epilogue vs 22.2 resident, and the epilogue adds ~2.4
(8 MB of bf16 partials through the reduce-add). In-prepare: pooler r6 28.58 vs rq 29.31, qkv 20.77 vs 20.80, unpooler 27.26 vs
29.86, o 11.30 vs 15.04. cuBLAS at m=256 uses 128×64 tiles with full K (no split-K tail) and reaches 70–79% of peak. A winning
m=256 design needs: no synchronized split-K tail, operand delivery that does not re-read x 32× through L2, and ≥ 85% tensor
utilisation with the decode in the loop. Nobody owns this now; verify32 has the lowest leverage (6.3), so it is last in line.

## Open ideas not yet measured (in priority order)
1. k128 merged stages / one barrier pair per two k64 rounds (P4's bf16 pipeline fix gave −2.4 µs at m=256 on the box) — P4 is on it in rs128p.
2. Stream-K grid on 132 SMs (author measuring).
3. x multicast across a 2-CTA cluster (the two K-split neighbours of the same x k-slice) — cluster launch costs ~1–2 µs fixed here, so it must also drop per-SM ingest (author measuring).
4. Epilogue: start the reduce-add of the first 64-row half while the second warpgroup finishes (−0.15 measured; not worth it alone); a 2-CTA/SM layout to stagger tails (unmeasured).
5. unpooler m=128: rq ties r6 (22.66 vs 22.69); any ≥ 0.5 µs win above flips it (leverage 8.2 → ≈ −4 score-µs per 0.5).
