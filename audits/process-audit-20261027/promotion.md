No evidence here establishes an official requirement for every local proof, independent narrative, history regeneration or duplicate review.

Use a shorter screen→verify→stack→submit path:

1. **Screen:** one reusable bounded harness, touched shapes versus the actual current selected incumbent; validate numerical output, state/counters and cleanup. Keep all signed observations. No minimum gain, confidence interval separation, or agreement-of-estimators gate.
2. **Verify:** one independent review of changed source and harness delta. Reuse unchanged hash-bound proofs. Preserve raw reporting fields before guards; repair reporting-only defects without re-proving unchanged kernels.
3. **Stack:** retain promising mechanisms, build one immutable combined package, run actual prepare and full-policy validation once against current best. Record selected paths. Use mixed evidence to choose a bounded discriminator, not an indefinite HOLD or a family ban.
4. **Submit:** official CHECK on the validated package, then scored submission promptly when its measured point improves, under existing user authorization. Treat package outcome separately from isolated mechanism causality. Update prose/catalogs after release, without making extra retrospective audits a promotion dependency.

The records support simplifying and caching the process. They do not support discarding numerical/lifetime/cleanup checks, promising additive gains, or fabricating how much faster the project would have shipped under the simpler path.
